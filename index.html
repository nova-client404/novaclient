Everything you need, nothing you don't

Nova Client focuses on useful client-side features while staying easy on your resources.

📊

Flexible HUD

FPS, CPS, ping, coordinates, facing, speed, clock, RAM and combo counter. Plus keystrokes, armor, effects and item counters. Choose the corner and scale freely.

❤️

Health Indicator

Shows health under nametags as a number, hearts, percentage or bar. Dynamic colors, absorption, range and filters for players, hostile mobs and animals.

🔍

Smooth Zoom

Zoom from 1.5× up to 50×, adjustable with the mouse wheel. Animated transitions, automatically reduced sensitivity and an optional cinematic mode.

🖱️

Auto Clicker

Configurable CPS ranges for left and right click, toggle or hold mode, presets (Legit, Normal, Fast), an auto-stop timer and a panic key.

💡

Fullbright

Client-side night vision without potions – no packets are sent to the server.

🏃

Toggle Sprint & Sneak

Automatic sprinting and toggleable sneaking for more relaxed movement.

💀

Death Coordinates

After you die, your coordinates and dimension appear right in chat – never lose your items again.

⚙️

Config GUI

Every setting is available in-game via sliders and toggles, stored in a clean JSON file.

Default keybinds

All keys can be rebound under Controls → Nova Client.

GToggle / hold auto clicker
R-ShiftOpen config GUI
CZoom (hold)
EndPanic: stop clicker & zoom

Installation in 4 steps

Nova Client runs on the Fabric Loader.

  1. Install the Fabric Loader for Minecraft 1.21.4 (loader ≥ 0.16.0) with Java 21.
  2. Download the Fabric API and place it in your mods folder.
  3. Put novaclient-1.0.0.jar into .minecraft/mods as well.
  4. Launch the game and press R-Shift to open the config GUI.
